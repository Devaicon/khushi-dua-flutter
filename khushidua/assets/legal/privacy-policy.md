# Privacy Policy

**Khushi Dua**
Effective date: 6 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Khushi Dua ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you use the Khushi Dua mobile application (the "App") on Android and iOS.

By using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Information you give us

If you create an account or sign in, we collect:

- **Name** and **email address**, when you sign up with email and password or sign in with Google.
- **Avatar choice**: the illustration (boy or girl) you pick for your profile. We do not collect profile photos.

You can use most of the App as a guest without giving us any personal information.

### 1.2 Information created while you use the App

If you are signed in, we store:

- **Listening progress**: which duas you have listened to all the way through. We use it to unlock sections and show your progress.
- **Points** earned in the App.
- **Membership status**, and whether an account has been restricted for misuse.

### 1.3 Information from your device

- **Location (only with your permission).** We use your device's location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. These calculations happen **on your device**. We do not send your location to our servers and we do not store it. To show your city name, the App uses your phone's built-in location service (provided by Apple or Google). If you refuse location access, the App uses a default location instead.
- **Microphone (only with your permission, and only when you use "Practice").** When you record yourself reciting a dua, the recording (up to 29 seconds) and the dua's Arabic text are sent to our recitation-checking service to produce feedback. Our service does not keep recordings after returning the result.
- **Notification token.** If you allow notifications, the App receives a push notification token from Firebase Cloud Messaging. If you are signed in, we save it with your account so we can send you announcements.
- **Settings stored on your device**, such as your language, age group, reminder times, text size and downloaded dua audio. These stay on your device.

### 1.4 Information collected by third parties

- **No advertising.** The App shows no ads and contains no advertising service.
- **Donations.** The App's "Donate" buttons open LearningSouls' donation page (https://learningsouls.org/donate), either inside the App or in your browser. Anything you enter on that page goes to LearningSouls and its payment provider under their own privacy terms; the App does not see or store it.
- **Firebase (Google).** We use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Messaging to run the App. Google processes data on our behalf under its terms: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

We do **not** sell your personal information.

---

## 2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- create and manage your account, and let you sign in;
- save your listening progress and points, and unlock sections;
- calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction;
- give feedback on your recitation when you use Practice;
- send the prayer and Azkar reminders you switch on, and announcements if you allow notifications;
- remind you once a week about supporting LearningSouls, unless you turn that reminder off;
- keep the App secure and prevent misuse;
- fix problems and improve the App.

---

## 3. Children's privacy

The App offers content for young children, older children and adults. We take extra care with children's information:

- A child can use the App as a guest without giving any personal information.
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) without a parent's or guardian's consent. We encourage parents to create and manage the account.
- The App shows no ads to anyone.
- A parent or guardian can delete their child's account from Settings in the App, or ask us to review or delete their child's information using the contact details below.

If you believe a child has given us personal information without consent, contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 4. Sharing information

We share information only:

- with the **service providers** listed in section 1.4 (Google Firebase), who process it to provide their services;
- when **required by law**, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or us;
- as part of a **merger, acquisition or sale** of the App. In that case we will notify you before your information falls under a different privacy policy.

When you share a dua image, the App uses your device's share sheet. You choose where it goes, and we do not receive a copy.

---

## 5. Data retention

- We keep account information for as long as your account exists.
- When you delete your account, your profile, points, listening progress, notification token and messages sent to you are deleted immediately and permanently.
- Location is not stored. Practice recordings are not kept after the result is returned.

---

## 6. Your rights and choices

- **Delete your account:** you can delete your account at any time from **Settings → Delete account** in the App. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. If you can no longer sign in, email us and we will delete it for you.
- **Permissions:** you can turn off location, microphone and notification access at any time in your device settings.
- **Access and correction:** you can ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, by emailing ghazanfar.shah@learningsouls.org.
- Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have further rights, such as objecting to processing or data portability. Contact us to use them. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

---

## 7. Security

Account and content data is sent to and stored by Google Firebase over encrypted connections, and access to it is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 8. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, those transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses.

---

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Effective date" above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the App. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
